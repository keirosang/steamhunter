## 📌 官方内容重点解析
根据官方发布的公告信息，本次调整重点聚焦于以下关键维度：

- ## **DREGS 1.0 Is Here!**
- **DREGS is officially leaving Early Access today!** And this isn't just another update. We've fundamentally changed what DREGS is about, how you play it, and, most importantly, how we want you to feel while playing it.A few weeks ago, we shared our plans to rethink the core experience of DREGS. Today, we're delivering on that promise, including one more major change that we haven't talked about until now. There is a LOT to cover, so let's get into it.### **Why We Changed DREGS**
- When we launched DREGS into Early Access, we had a game that we knew could be a lot of fun with friends. Exploring abandoned spaceships, solving ridiculous puzzles, encountering horrifying creatures, and watching your friends meet increasingly unfortunate ends made for some pretty memorable moments. But something wasn't quite right. We spent time talking to players who enjoyed DREGS, but we also sought out people who we thought *should* enjoy it, yet had decided not to play it. The feedback was illuminating. While players generally liked what they saw, many felt DREGS looked too similar to other co-op games they already owned. The familiar loop of entering a dangerous location, collecting valuable junk, and escaping before time runs out wasn't offering them something different enough to justify picking up another game. And we agreed.We also heard something else, particularly from people who had already played DREGS: the mission timer was making the game stressful in a way that wasn't necessarily fun. Players wanted to explore, experiment with the environments, solve puzzles, and generally mess around with their friends without a clock constantly telling them to hurry up.That got us thinking about what made DREGS special in the first place. It wasn't the junk. It wasn't the timer. It was the derelicts themselves, the strange things you could do in them, and the hilarious situations that arose when a group of friends tried to work together.**So we decided to keep everything we loved about DREGS and build a different game around it.**### **From Salvaging to Cleaning and Restoration**
- This is the big one.**You're no longer primarily a crew of scavengers trying to grab as much junk as possible. You're now a crew tasked with cleaning up and restoring horribly infested derelict spacecraft.**And what better way to deal with a horrifying alien fungal infestation than with a flamethrower? Yes. Everyone gets a flamethrower now.When you board a derelict, you'll find that it's already overrun with xeno-fungal growths, which we've been calling Creep. Previously, this infestation would spread as you spent more time in a mission. Now, you're arriving to find the place in a truly disgusting state. Your job is to clean it up.Armed with your trusty Monkey-Bar (part monkey wrench, part crowbar) and your new flamethrower, you'll work together to purge the infestation, restore the derelict's systems, explore its secrets, and complete the many puzzles and challenges hidden throughout.There's something remarkably satisfying about turning a disgusting, alien-infested corridor into a relatively clean, respectable corridor. We've found ourselves spending time clearing every last bit of Creep out of rooms just because it feels good. Of course, these derelicts are still dangerous. There are still monsters, lethal machinery, traps, and an impressive number of opportunities to accidentally kill yourself or your friends. But the overall experience is different.**Instead of constantly rushing to get in and get out, we want you to enjoy the process of putting these broken places back together.**Take your time. Explore. Figure out how things work. Solve the puzzles. Set something on fire that you probably shouldn't have. It's a different kind of fun, and we think it suits DREGS much better.### **A New Way to Score: Cleanliness Pays!**
