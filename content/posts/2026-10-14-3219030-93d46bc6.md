Progressing further into Career will not advance your Franchise accreditation. In Career Mode, your Accreditation Tier is increased by gaining medals from scenario zoos and Wildlife Reserves. In Franchise Mode, increase your Accreditation Tier by having zoos with a high Zoo Reputation, and Wildlife Reserves with a high Biodiversity Rating.  We also want to take a moment to share some essential resources, and tackle some of the questions you’ve been asking as you’ve taken your first steps into the game.  #### **Guides**
